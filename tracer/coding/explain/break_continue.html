Two statements change the normal flow inside a loop. continue abandons the rest of the current round and moves on to the next one. break leaves the loop completely, and execution continues after it.

They keep loops simple: skip the items you do not care about, stop as soon as the answer is found. This loop also shows the for-each form, which visits each element of a collection without an index.

Common mistake

Expecting break to leave every loop: it only leaves the innermost one. To stop nested loops, use a flag, or put them in a function and return.
