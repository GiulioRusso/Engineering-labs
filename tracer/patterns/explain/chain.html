Chain of Responsibility

Problem. Support tickets go to whoever can deal with them: a bot answers easy questions, an agent fixes accounts, engineers fix bugs. If the app decided who gets what, it would need to know every team and their rules.

Solution. Link the handlers in a chain, each with a pointer to the next. The app gives every ticket to the first handler. Each handler either resolves it or passes it along. Adding, removing or reordering handlers changes only how the chain is linked.

  1. A Handler base with a next pointer and handle().
  2. Each subclass says what it can handle and how.
  3. handle(): deal with it, or forward it to next.

Where you meet it

Event bubbling in the browser DOM (a click goes to the button, then its parents), middleware pipelines, exception handlers searched outward through the call stack, logging levels.

Common mistakes

Requests that fall off the end unhandled, with nobody noticing; put a catch-all at the end. And long chains that make it hard to see who handled what.
