Vectors and vector fields

Dot and cross products

The dot product measures how much two vectors point the same way: the length of one times the shadow of the other on it. The cross product is a vector at right angles to both, as long as the area of the parallelogram they span.

$$\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b = |\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\cos\theta, \qquad |\mathbf a\times\mathbf b| = |\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\sin\theta$$

A gravitational field

Every point of space gets an arrow: the pull a 1 kg test mass would feel there. Fields of several masses simply add.

$$\mathbf g = -\sum_i \frac{G M_i}{r_i^2}\,\hat{\mathbf r}_i = -\nabla V, \qquad V = -\sum_i \frac{G M_i}{r_i}$$

Divergence and curl

Put a tiny circle in the flow. The net flow out of it, per unit area, is the divergence: is the point a source or a sink? The flow around it, per unit area, is the curl: would a little paddle wheel spin? The tracer dots show both: they spread out, or they turn.

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf F = \lim_{A\to 0}\frac{1}{A}\oint \mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\ell, \qquad (\nabla\times\mathbf F)_z = \lim_{A\to 0}\frac{1}{A}\oint \mathbf F\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell$$
