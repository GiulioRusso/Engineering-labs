Functions

A function gives one output \(y\) for each input \(x\). Drag the round handles: the graph, its equation and its key points follow.

Linear: a constant rate

Two points fix a line. Its slope is the rise over the run, the same everywhere.

$$y = m x + q, \qquad m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$

Quadratic: a parabola

The vertex form shows the turning point \((h, k)\) directly; \(a\) sets how steep and which way it opens. The discriminant counts the real roots.

$$y = a(x-h)^2 + k, \qquad \Delta = b^2 - 4ac, \qquad x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt\Delta}{2a}$$

Exponential and logarithm

An exponential multiplies by the same factor for every step in \(x\): it never reaches zero. The logarithm undoes it: its graph is the mirror image across \(y = x\).

$$y = A\,b^x \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x = \log_b \frac{y}{A}, \qquad \log_b b = 1$$
